# Spotswood Sea Serpents: standing rules

Website for the Spotswood Sea Serpents summer swim team: homepage with meet cards, swimmer stats, photo gallery, and an admin area for meets, results, and photos. The redesign spec is in `design/` (HANDOFF.md, PROMPTS.md, mockups/, fixtures/). The mockups are a spec, not code to copy.

`design/` and `sample-data/` hold real kids' names, times, and photos. Both are gitignored: never commit, push, or publish them, and keep real names out of code comments and commit messages too.

## How to work
- Use the existing stack: static HTML pages, Azure Functions (Node; one folder per function with function.json and index.js), Azure Table and Blob Storage, Azure Static Web Apps. Do not add frameworks, databases, or services without asking.
- Plan first and wait for approval before editing. One step per branch, reviewed on its pull request's preview link. Merging to main deploys the live site.
- Do not invent data. Ask if something is not in the results sheets, the admin form, or the mockups.
- Mockup-only items must never ship: the dark "Mock controls" bars, sample data (Highland Divers meet, 84° forecast, "Test Pool", example swimmers), Win/Loss and Forecast switches, placeholder gallery tags, "Sample numbers", "mock today", fake delays, base64 images, and `href="#"` links.
- Treat numbers in design/fixtures/EXPECTED.md as cross-checks, not truth. Investigate differences. (Its relay counts leave out exhibition relays; the importer's include them.)
- Keep this file current: when a change sets or changes a standing rule (a design, content, or data decision the user made), update CLAUDE.md in the same commit. It's the only memory every session shares, on any computer or phone; notes saved anywhere else don't carry over.
- Never name an Azure Function folder starting with "admin". Admin pages and APIs are protected by route rules in staticwebapp.config.json (the "administrator" role).

## Design rules
- Fonts: Poppins (headings, big numbers), Inter (body).
- Colors: brand green #1A6B2E, ink #10240F, muted #5B6B58, gold #F2B327 with ink #2A1E00, soft tint #F4F7E4 (border #DCE3C4), gold tint #FFF6D8 (border #EACB6A). Page background white (2026-10-20, option A; it was a #D9E8BC to #FAFBF4 fade).
- White cards (28px radius) on the white page, set off by a #DCE3C4 border and a soft, fairly deep shadow (deepened at the user's choice, 2026-10-20). The header is white on every page and screen size, its logo and menu centered top to bottom (2026-10-20), with no line under it (a soft-tint band was tried and removed, the user's choice, 2026-10-20). Insets inside cards use the soft tint with a thin border.
- Gold means celebration only (WIN chip, Most improved, 1st places, PB, podium numbers, Biggest drop). Green means data, actions, and links.
- No red on pages parents and kids see. Losses use a muted gray-green. Red is for admin Delete only.
- Never rely on color alone. Always include text or an icon.
- Dialogs: white, centered on desktop (max 520px), bottom sheet under 600px, Esc closes, focus returns to the trigger, Tab stays inside, page scroll locked, `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal`. The photo viewer is the one exception on width: up to 1040px on desktop, with the photo as tall as the window allows (the user's choice, 2026-10-13).
- Confirmations (delete a meet, remove or reject a photo) use the site's own dialog, never the browser's `confirm()` or `alert()`.
- Page content lines up with the header's logo: every page uses the same `.wrap` container, and a page with narrower content (Stats, Schedule, Admin) keeps that width on the left rather than centering it.
- Focus ring: 3px gold. Target WCAG AA contrast.
- Animation (2026-10-20): short, once, never looping, and none at all when the device has Reduce motion on. So far: a swimmer's event chart draws its line left to right the first time it comes into view, each swim's dot and time appearing as the line reaches it and the gold best popping in last; new badges pop in with a gold shine the first time a device shows them for that swimmer (remembered in the browser only); tapping a badge turns its tile over as it grows into the badge popup, which turns back into the tile on close (a swipe down on a phone just slides the sheet away); the favorite star bounces with a gold ring when a swimmer is saved; the photo upload's thank-you check mark draws itself; and on the Stats page's leaderboards, switching the age group, Girls or Boys, or the event glides each swimmer from their old place to their new one, with swimmers new to the list fading in (2026-10-21); also (2026-10-21) photos in the rows fade in as each one loads, the green line under the leaderboards' Girls and Boys tabs slides to the picked tab, a swimmer's team record "how close" bar fills from the left the first time it comes into view, the leaderboards' "Show all" eases the list open with the added swimmers fading in one after another, and the photo viewer's Previous and Next buttons and arrow keys slide the photos the way a swipe does. A homepage motto reveal (with a slow photo zoom) was tried and removed, and score count-ups and PB-tag glints were left out (the user's choice, 2026-10-21).
- Homepage photo (2026-10-20): on phones, a team photo right under the header, the full width of the window with square corners, trimmed 40px from the top (the pennants) with its bottom unchanged (2026-10-20), its text lined up with the logo; wider screens keep it a rounded card inside the page width, as wide as the cards (the full-width photo looked too wide there, the user's choice, 2026-10-20). On it, over a dark blue-gray fade at its bottom: the team motto "Hard work pays off!", stacked in capitals with "HARD WORK" in white and "PAYS OFF!" in gold, centered on phones and on the left wider than a phone (the payoff, so gold stays a celebration; 2026-10-20), and, on screens wider than a phone, a "Next meet: June 16 vs. Stafford" button that jumps to the next-meet card below (hidden when no meet is coming up); phones show only the motto (2026-10-20, the user's choice). No team name on the photo, since the header carries it; the page's heading, "Spotswood Sea Serpents", is for screen readers only. The homepage has no "Share a photo" button (removed 2026-10-20); photos are shared from the Photos page, whose upload dialog lives in assets/upload.js. Served from assets/hero-800, hero-1600, and hero-2000 (WebP with a JPEG fallback, resized, stripped of metadata, saturation raised 10% (backed down from 30%), and cooled slightly toward blue, at the user's choice, 2026-10-20).

## Content rules
- "vs." means home and "at" means away. Titles use the short name ("vs. Massad"); score rows use the full name ("Curtis Park Seahawks 511").
- Dates: the full month and day everywhere, like "July 22" and "July 13 · Final". Times: "6:00pm".
- Stat labels in the meet popup (update 4): "Top-3 finishes" ("26 were 1st"), "Personal bests" ("by 74 swimmers"), "Point scorers" ("of 109 swimmers"), "Time dropped" ("2:56", "off best times"). The score banner always reads "Final team score" (update 5).

## Data rules
- Times are stored as seconds. 50yd and 50m are different events.
- Results sheets are saved for both teams, so a meet's full results can be shown. Stats, swimmer pages, and the swimmer list are Spotswood only. A tie for first counts as a win. Exhibition is not scored.
- Team scores are added up from the sheet's points on import. The admin can type a score in, and an import never overwrites a typed-in score.
- Most improved: the biggest percent drop below seed time across a swimmer's swims, at least 2%, shown in seconds with the event. (Not shown anywhere since 2026-10-07, when the user dropped the swimmer page's stat strip because the badges covered most of it.) Personal best: faster than the swimmer's previous best in that event, meaning their earlier swims on the site. A first swim in an event is not a PB, even when it beats the seed time (the user's choice, 2026-10-07). Barrier Breaker and Time Dropper still measure against seed times.
- Badges follow design/update-2/UPDATE.md, section 3, as changed by design/update-4/UPDATE.md, sections C to E: no levels; a badge that counts something lists every instance, newest first, and its pill is that list's count or total; Meet Attendance; a fixed display order with New first. Every Spotswood swimmer's badges are worked out again after each import or meet delete and saved in the SwimmerBadges table. Only legal, timed swims count (Meet Attendance also counts a meet with a DQ); each relay leg is 25 m (yards converted). Anchor counts only 100m relays, since the sheets list 4 of a 125m relay's 5 swimmers.
- Converted seeds (update 4, rule A): a seed equal to the swimmer's own earlier time in the other course x 1.11 was converted between yards and meters; the importer flags it, and it's left out of every comparison with the seed.
- The meet popup's Personal bests tile counts PBs the site's way (against earlier swims), not "faster than seed". Point scorers and Top point scorers use individual points only. Points by age group (update 5) uses the sheet's points column: All adds up to the team score, and Boys and Girls together do too; an 8 & under relay's points are split among its listed swimmers by their own age. Biggest time drops (update 5) are one per age group, all ages, chosen by percent below a real seed and shown in seconds; slow seeds aren't filtered out, since every one checked in 2026 was the swimmer's own earlier time.
- Opponent names come from the admin meet record, not the PDF.
- Championship meets (the division finals, every team in the division; 2026-10-12): set as Meet type "Championship" with a name instead of an opponent. Events are swum in groups A, B, and C (called "groups" on the site, at the user's choice, 2026-10-12; the code and stored rows call them flights); a swimmer's place is ranked by official time across all groups (a tie shares a place), with the place in their group kept alongside ("1st in B group"). Points are the sheet's, as printed, and count toward badges and season points. The standings come from the sheet's Overall Combined table; the card shows Spotswood's place ("5th of 6", a gold CHAMPIONS for a win) and the popup's banner reads "Final team standings", with a gold CHAMPIONS chip only for a win (its place is plain from the list). Its yards events stay separate from the season's meters events.
- Team records (2026-10-12): loaded on the admin Records tab from a saved copy of SwimTopia's Team Records page (meters only; events, times, years, the converted-time "*", and holders; holders show on the admin Records tab only, never on public pages, at the user's choice, 2026-10-12). An import marks a Spotswood swim or relay strictly faster than the record before that meet as a team record, whether or not the admin lets it update the stored records (the import's checkbox, on by default). A record set here remembers the one it replaced, so a re-import compares against that and a meet delete puts it back; loading the page again keeps a faster or equal record set here. Records are meters; a yards swim (a yards meet, or the championship) counts at its time x 1.11, rounded to the hundredth, as SwimTopia converts it, and a record it sets is saved as converted ("*") and shown with its yards time ("from 21.22 yd"); yards times still stay out of season bests, PBs, and leaderboards (2026-10-13). For meets imported before the records were loaded, the Records tab's "Match this season's records" marks the Spotswood swim with exactly each record's time from the latest season, or for a converted record the yards swim that converts to it (no old record is known for those, so none is shown). Shown as: a gold "Team record" tag on the swim, a "Team record holder" box on the swimmer's event card, a "how close" bar when their season best is within 5%, the Record Breaker badge (first in the order), a gold "N new team records" pill on the meet card, and a "New team record" card in the meet popup.
- Leaderboards (2026-10-13): on the Stats page under the "Find a swimmer" card (titled like the Leaderboards card), replacing the season totals card, shown until a swimmer is picked. For an age group (as the event names give it; chips youngest first, a band before the "& Under" group ending at the same age: 6 & Under, 7–8, 8 & Under, 9–10; the row fades at its right edge while there's more to scroll), Girls or Boys (no All tab, the user's choice, 2026-10-13), and an individual meters event: each Spotswood swimmer's best legal time in the latest season, fastest first, ties sharing a place, with "Age 10 · July 8 vs. Massad" under the name (their age on that meet's sheet); top 8, then "Show all". The record itself isn't shown above the list (removed at the user's choice, 2026-10-13; never its holder in any case), but a swimmer who holds the current team record gets the gold "Team record" tag, and favorites are starred. Relays and the championship's yards events aren't included. Saved with the badges after each import or meet delete.
- Re-importing a meet replaces its results.
- The next-meet card's "Map" button (2026-10-20, was "Directions") pins the pool in Google Maps by its name and address rather than starting a route from the viewer's location.
- Next-meet forecast (2026-10-17): covers the hours the meet runs, from warm-up (or the start when there's no warm-up time) until four hours after the start, from the Weather Service's hourly forecast. The chip shows the warm-up temperature and the worst weather in those hours; the note is one summary line like "84° at warm-up, cooling to 75°. Storms possible, most likely around 7pm." No hour-by-hour strip (the user's choice). Without the meet's times, or past the hourly forecast's reach, it falls back to the day's forecast.
- Photos: nobody is asked who they are, and no uploader name is stored or shown (the user's choice, 2026-10-13). Nothing goes live before review. A photo's meet comes from the date it was taken, read on the server without exposing the photo's GPS location. The time it was taken is saved too, but only to order photos (newest day first, each day's photos in the order taken) and to flag a pending photo as a duplicate (same time to the second and same size as another); it's never shown or sent to a page (the user's choice, 2026-10-13).
