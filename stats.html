How badges work

Badges are checked every time results are imported. Only legal swims count; DQs, no-shows and scratches never earn or remove anything (Meet Attendance still counts a meet where a swim ended in a DQ). The pill on a badge always shows the swimmer’s real total or count. A badge is marked New at the meet where it is first earned, and again at any later meet that changes its pill.
